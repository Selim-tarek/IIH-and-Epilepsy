## 2. Cohort structure

- 3,601 IIH cases, 9,852 controls. 2,732 cases enter the matched analysis;
  **869 cases are dropped** (`in_matched_analysis == 0`, `match_set` blank).
  Of those 869: 304 have no BMI, 45 are male, 14 had a presenting seizure.
  No documented reason variable exists for the drop.
- 2,732 matched sets, no control reused, no orphan sets.
- **Matching is variable-ratio 1:1 to 1:4, not 1:4.** Set sizes:
  1 control × 34 sets, 2 × 168, 3 × 638, 4 × 1,892 (mean 3.60).
- **Matching tolerance is confounded with sex.** `match_tier`:

  | tier | F controls | M controls |
  |---|---|---|
  | `0_full` | 6,773 | 1,318 |
  | `2_bmi5` / `3_year3` / `4_age5` (BMI ±5, year ±3, age ±5) | 1,653 | 0 |
  | `1_bmi5` / `2_age5` / `3_wide` | 0 | 108 |

  Female sets were relaxed on BMI/year/age; male sets on BMI/age, and per the
  FINAL README male matching used **no calendar-year constraint at all**.
  Sex-stratified estimates therefore rest on different matching designs.
- Achieved balance on the three matched variables is good (SMD ≤ 0.03).

## 3. The central structural problem: variables are cohort-specific

Cases and controls were abstracted from different pipelines and stacked.
Availability is almost perfectly complementary:

| Available **for cases only** | Available **for controls only** |
|---|---|
| `op_cmh2o`, `op_first_lp`, `op_max` | `race`, `ethnicity` |
| `osa`, `htn`, `pcos`, `smoking`, `alcohol`, `bariatric` | `dob` |
| `enceph_*`, `empty_sella`, `globe_flat`, `onsd_distend`, `sinus_sten`, `skullbase_thin`, `csf_leak`, `mrv_done` | `last_encounter_date`, `death_date` |
| `shunt`, `stent` | `enc_pre12`, `enc_post` |
| `eeg_*`, `temporal_onset`, `concordant` | `outcome_criterion`, `pnes_ever` |
| `seizure_ever`, `presenting_seizure` | `excl_prior_seizure`, `excl_prior_asm` |

Direct consequences:

1. **No confounder is adjustable across groups.** OSA, hypertension, PCOS,
   smoking and alcohol exist for cases and are 100% missing for controls, so
   the protocol's residual-confounding adjustment cannot be run at all. Only
   age, BMI, sex (the matched variables) are shared.
2. **Race/ethnicity matching cannot be verified** — cases have no race field,
   so the protocol's "race/ethnicity exact where recorded" cannot be checked
   or reported.
3. **Surveillance intensity cannot be compared.** `enc_pre12`/`enc_post` exist
   only for controls, so the protocol's core detection-bias sensitivity
   analysis (adjust for encounter/imaging counts) is not executable.
4. **The outcome algorithm cannot be shown to have been applied identically.**
   `outcome_criterion` (1 = two codes ≥30 d apart, 2 = one code + ASM, 88 = no
   event) is populated for all 9,852 controls and for **zero** cases.
   `pnes_ever` likewise. `excl_prior_seizure`/`excl_prior_asm` are populated
   for controls only. Whether cases and controls were run through the same
   two-code/ASM logic is currently unverifiable — and this is the estimand.

## 4. Person-time and censoring — the largest threat to the primary result

- Control follow-up **reproduces exactly** (9,852/9,852 within 0.01 y) as
  `min(last_encounter_date, seizure_date, death_date) − index_date`.
- Case follow-up **cannot be reproduced at all**: `last_encounter_date` and
  `death_date` are missing for all 2,732 cases. The rule used for case
  person-time is undocumented and unverifiable. Implied case end dates run to
  **2031-01-11**.
- **1,046 controls are censored at a `last_encounter_date` in the future**
  (max 2034-04-16). These are almost certainly scheduled appointments, not
  observed contacts. They inflate control person-time, deflate the control
  rate, and inflate the hazard ratio.
- Median follow-up 4.7 y (cases) vs 3.4 y (controls) — a 1.3-year asymmetry
  produced by two different censoring rules, not by design.
- **Zero deaths among 2,732 cases vs 151 among 9,852 controls.** Death was
  ascertained for controls only. The FINAL workbook's Table 6 ("competing
  risk of death does not alter incidence estimates", 0 case deaths) is an
  artifact of missing case death data, not a finding.

## 6. Missing-data codes

`88` and `99` are distinct and must not be collapsed:
`99` = unknown after searching; `88` = not applicable / no event
(`outcome_criterion = 88` means no event; `enceph_index = 88` appears to mean
not assessed — Q5). Blank (`NaN`) here means **not abstracted for this cohort
arm**, i.e. structural, and must not be imputed.

Informative (non-88/99, non-blank) counts among the 2,732 matched cases:

| Variable | Informative | 99 = unknown |
|---|---|---|
| `enceph_index` | **71 (2.6%)** — 52 positive, 19 negative | 0 (2,661 coded 88) |
| `skullbase_thin` | 32 (1.2%) | 2,700 |
| `globe_flat` | 321 | 2,411 |
| `sinus_sten` | 815 | 1,917 |
| `empty_sella` | 747 | 1,985 |
| `onsd_distend` | 724 | 2,008 |
| `eeg_done` | 445 | 2,287 |
| `eeg_epileptiform` | 252 | 2,480 |

**The mediation aim is not identifiable.** Encephalocele status is known for
2.6% of cases and for 0% of controls; there is no imaging-adequacy flag, no
baseline-vs-follow-up imaging pair, and no serial imaging dates. A natural
direct/indirect effects decomposition on 71 assessed patients with 52 exposed
is not defensible, and neither is treating the 2,661 `88` codes as negative.

## 7. Variables the protocol requires that are absent entirely

Frisén grade; perimetry mean deviation; fulminant phenotype; symptom-onset-to-
diagnosis interval; congenital-vs-acquired encephalocele; imaging adequacy;
**all medication data** (no ASM, no acetazolamide/topiramate/zonisamide, no
start/stop dates); treatment dates for shunt/stent (binary only); drug-resistant
epilepsy; Engel class; lesionectomy; new encephalocele on follow-up MRI; GERD;
site/campus; chart-review adjudication results; the 20% IIH double-adjudication
sample; the 15% imaging double-read sample; the 200 algorithm-negative
validation reviews.

Therefore, as the data stand: **no time-varying treatment model or marginal
structural model** (§4 of the brief), **no severity gradient** beyond a crude
shunt/stent-derived proxy, **no algorithm validation, PPV, sensitivity, or
two-phase correction**, **no inter-rater kappa**, and **no reverse cohort**.
